Investigator Agreement · Preview

  1. Independent professional: Status, scope and any employment or contractor relationship must be stated in the final written agreement.
  2. Qualifications: The investigator is responsible for maintaining any licences, registrations and permissions required by local law. The platform must verify claims before live assignments.
  3. Lawful methods: No unlawful surveillance, trespass, unauthorized account/device access, harassment, coercion or privacy violations.
  4. Confidentiality and data: Protect case information, use it only for authorized work, and follow applicable data-protection and retention requirements.
  5. Fees and deliverables: Assignment scope, deadline, compensation, approved expenses, acceptance criteria and payment timing must be agreed in writing before each case.
  6. Termination and disputes: The final agreement must define cancellation, complaints, liability, governing law and dispute resolution.
  7. Prototype notice: This draft is not legal advice, a job offer, or a digitally signed contract. Local legal review is required before use.
